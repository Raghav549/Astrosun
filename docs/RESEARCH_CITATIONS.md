# AstroSun Research Citations

This document records the research basis used to shape the scientific architecture.

## Core references

### Modern astrodynamics
**Brahe: A Modern Astrodynamics Library for Research and Engineering Applications** — arXiv:2601.06452 (2026).

Relevant concepts: IAU/IERS time and reference-frame conventions, Earth-orbit force models, atmospheric drag, solar radiation pressure, third-body perturbations, SGP propagation, coordinate conversion, Keplerian state representations and visibility computation.

### Computational astrophysics, data science and AI/ML
**Computational Astrophysics, Data Science & AI/ML in Astronomy: A Perspective from Indian Community** — arXiv:2501.03876 (2025).

Relevant concepts: HPC, GPU computing, astronomical data growth, reproducible/FAIR data, AI/ML for images/light curves/spectra, simulation-to-observation workflows, gravitational-wave analysis, solar/heliospheric modelling, numerical relativity and astronomy education.

### Gravitational physics in Indian astronomy
**Gravitational physics in the context of Indian astronomy: A vision document** — arXiv:2501.04333 (2025).

Relevant concepts: gravitational-wave astronomy, pulsar timing, black-hole imaging, compact-object physics, numerical relativity, GRMHD, future observatories and Indian research priorities.

## Implementation rule

Papers inform algorithms and research modules, but a citation does not automatically establish that every claim made by a model is scientifically validated. AstroSun must preserve the difference between published evidence, numerical calculation, model inference and traditional Jyotisha interpretation.
